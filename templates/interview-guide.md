# Requirement gathering interview guide

## How to use this template

Use this with one person, for 30 to 60 minutes. It is a script and a question bank, not the notes. Record what they actually say in [meeting notes](meeting-notes.md) or a copy of the notes section at the bottom.

Talk to the person who does the task, not only the person who owns the policy. In a leave process that is often the HR officer who updates the spreadsheet on Friday, and one supervisor who approves from a phone.

> **Guidance.** Delete this section before you file the notes. Keep the questions you actually asked.

## 1. Before the session

- Read the last policy, the spreadsheet, and five real requests. Write down what you think happens. You are about to have that story corrected.
- Tell the person the purpose, the duration, and that you want exceptions, not the happy path.
- Prepare three real cases from their files, with names removed if the data is sensitive. "Walk me through this one" beats "how does leave work?"

## 2. Opening

- Purpose of the conversation, in one sentence.
- What you will do with the notes, and who will see them.
- Permission to ask about a real recent case.

## 3. Context

- What is your role in this process, in a normal week?
- Who else touches it, and what do they do that you do not?
- Which tool do you actually use when you are in a hurry?

## 4. Walk the real process

- Take the last request you handled. What arrived, from whom, and in what form?
- What did you look at before you decided?
- What did you write down, and where does that record live now?
- Who did you tell, and how?

Ask them to show you the screen or the chat. Watching one case is worth ten opinions about the process.

## 5. Exceptions and rules

- When do you say no?
- When do you make an exception, and who is allowed to?
- What is the rule for the awkward case: half days, overlapping teams, late notice, missing documents, the approver being away?
- Which of those rules is written, and which is "how we do it"?

Separate policy from habit. Both matter. Only the policy should become a `BR-` rule without a conversation.

## 6. Information and decisions

- What do you need on the request in order to decide?
- What do you record that nobody downstream uses?
- What do you wish you could see that you currently ask someone for?
- Who is allowed to see the sensitive parts?

## 7. Pain, volume, and priority

- How many of these in a normal week, and in the busiest week?
- Where does time go that you resent?
- What goes wrong that the employee feels?
- If we could only fix one thing before the next peak, what would it be?

## 8. Close

- Restate the process in five steps and let them correct you.
- "What did I not ask about?"
- Agree what you may quote, and what needs to be checked with someone else.
- Tell them what happens next and when they will see the notes.

## 9. After the session

- Write the notes the same day. Mark guesses as guesses.
- Pull candidate business rules into a list with the source ("Retno, 8 Oct, not yet in the company regulation").
- Send the notes to the person. Ask them to correct facts, not to rewrite your analysis.

## Question bank by topic

Use a handful. An interview that reads every line below is an interrogation.

| Topic | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | What makes someone start this? What happens if they never do? |
| Time | What is too late? What is too early? Which dates do not count? |
| Money or balance | What number changes, when does it change, and who can change it back? |
| Authority | Who can approve, who can override, and what happens if that person is absent? |
| Evidence | What proof is required, who keeps it, and for how long? |
| Privacy | Who must not see this, even if they ask? |
| Downstream | Who consumes the result, and what do they do by hand today? |
| Failure | Tell me about the last time this went wrong. What did you do that is not in the policy? |

## Notes for interviews in Indonesian SMEs

> **Catatan.** Di banyak UKM, proses yang "resmi" dan proses yang dijalankan lewat WhatsApp berbeda. Tanyakan keduanya. Jangan menerjemahkan `izin` menjadi `cuti` sebelum mereka menjelaskan apakah saldonya terpotong.

- The spreadsheet and the WhatsApp group are both evidence. Neither is a system of record until someone says which one wins when they disagree.
- A senior person in the room will answer for everyone. For a workshop, give the people who do the work a turn before the head of department summarises. For a one-to-one, do not invite the head of department unless the topic is policy.
- Public holidays and `cuti bersama` are not the same thing. Ask which dates are deducted from annual leave.
- Confirm whether a number is company policy or their memory of the labour law. Write it as policy, and say it has not had a legal review, unless counsel was in the room.
