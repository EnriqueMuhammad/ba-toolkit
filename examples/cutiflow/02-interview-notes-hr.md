## What she does in this process

Retno sets the leave policy and handles disputes. She does not update the balance sheet. That is Putri Lestari, the other person in HR. Retno sees leave when someone complains, when a supervisor forwards a screenshot, or when payroll asks who was unpaid.

## Process in her words

An employee messages their supervisor on WhatsApp. The supervisor replies "ok" or asks them to move the date. If HR is lucky, someone forwards the chat. Putri types the approved days into `Saldo_Cuti_2026.xlsx` on Friday. The sheet has one row per employee and columns for entitled days, cuti bersama, used days, and remaining days.

People ask Retno or Putri for their balance during the week, mostly on Thursday and Friday, because they do not trust Monday's number. Retno said she answers from memory or opens the sheet. There is no form.

Sick leave arrives as a photograph of a doctor's note in the same chat. Retno's phrase: "the photo lives in someone's phone, and I only know about it if they remember to forward it."

Cuti bersama is announced by HR. In 2026 Putri deducted four days from eligible employees by hand after the dates were agreed. Several people had already planned annual leave around those dates and were surprised the days came off their balance.

## Exceptions

- If Dimas is travelling, another manager replies in the chat. Retno knows that person may not be the assigned supervisor. She will accept a visible queue and no delegate for a pilot. She does not want to keep the chat.
- Finance has an informal rule that nobody in Finance takes annual leave in the last week of a quarter, because of the close. Budi enforces it by refusing in the chat. It is not in the spreadsheet.
- Putri sometimes corrects a balance weeks later when a screenshot appears. There is no comment on the cell. The old value is gone.

## Volume

Putri's estimate, which Retno asked us not to treat as a count: 8 to 15 leave requests in a normal week, higher in the weeks before cuti bersama. Retno timed two Fridays in September 2026 at about three hours each for the spreadsheet, including chasing people. She did not count the balance questions. She described them as "several a day".

## What good would look like, for her

Employees stop asking HR for the balance. Friday becomes adjustments and mistakes, not retyping the week's chats. Payroll receives a file of who was off, and HR stops calculating money. Sick-leave photos stop circulating in chat.

She does not want a mobile app. She does not want this project to touch payroll formulas.
